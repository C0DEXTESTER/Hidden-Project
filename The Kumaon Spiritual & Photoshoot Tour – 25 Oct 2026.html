Master timeline

Times are firm: the 9:15 AM and 4:25 PM boardings protect the whole schedule.

Stop 1 · Bhimtal & Bhimeshwar

Temple 6 AM – 7 PM · Sunrise 6:19 AM
  • 6:00 – 6:40 AM
    Sunrise on the Victorian dam

    Park at Daat by 6:00. Twilight from 5:55; mist and mirror reflections on the lake and island.

  • 6:40 – 7:30 AM
    Morning darshan

    17th-century Shiva shrine of King Baz Bahadur Chand. Quiet jalabhishek, no queue. Prasad is self-paid.

  • 7:30 – 8:30 AM
    Lake & courtyard photoshoot

    Group frame on the dam promenade, then portraits in the temple courtyard.

  • 8:30 – 9:15 AM
    Breakfast at Daat stalls

    Tea, bun-maska, parathas, Maggi. Order and pay your own.

  • 9:15 AM
    Everyone on the Traveller

    Beats the 11 AM Kainchi bottleneck and secures heavy-vehicle parking.

Stop 2 · Kainchi Dham

Ashram 7 AM – 7 PM
  • 10:15 – 11:30 AM
    Darshan & Hanuman Chalisa

    Leave phones, cameras and bags locked in the Traveller. No photography inside.

  • 11:30 AM – 12:15 PM
    Exterior photoshoot

    Red-and-white arch bridge, Kosi boulders, and the upper viewpoint 150 m up NH-109.

  • 12:15 PM
    Board and leave the valley

    Exit before the noon parking jam.

  • 12:35 – 1:15 PM
    Lunch halt · Bhowali Junction

    Multi-cuisine family restaurants. Order and pay your own.

Stop 3 · Nainital

Temple 5 AM – 7 PM
  • 1:45 – 2:00 PM
    Park & walk Mall Road

    Traveller parks at Tallital/Metropole; about 15 minutes’ walk to Mallital.

  • 2:00 – 2:35 PM
    Maa Naina Devi darshan

    Lakeside Shakti Peetha shrine.

  • 2:35 – 3:30 PM
    Naini Lake photoshoot

    Red dhvaja flags, temple archway, boat jetty with sailboats, Naina Peak behind.

  • 3:30 – 4:10 PM
    Bhotia (Tibetan) Market & Mall Road

    Free time for momos, coffee and shopping, all self-paid.

  • 4:10 – 4:25 PM
    Walk back & board by 4:25

    Strict boarding. Evening traffic is the only risk to the 6 PM drop.

Stop 4 · Haldwani Station

Station code HDW
  • 4:25 – 6:00 PM
    Descent via Jeolikote & Kathgodam

    75 minutes of driving plus a 20-minute buffer for city traffic.

  • 6:00 PM sharp
    Group drop at the main entrance

    Collect luggage from the rear boot before leaving the vehicle.

Where the 12 hours go: sightseeing & markets 6.5 hrs (54%) · transit 3.5 hrs (29%) · dining 2 hrs (17%).

Ground rules

Small things that keep the day on time.

Local vehicle only. Book a UK-registered Traveller. Out-of-state plates (DL, UP) pay ₹520 – 900/day extra and face stricter entry in Nainital.
Confirm the Nainital drop point. 20-seaters can’t use Mall Road in the afternoon; agree on Tallital or Metropole parking in advance.
One luggage bay. Keep train luggage and backpacks locked in the rear boot so everyone is hands-free all day.
Be early at 9:15 AM and 4:25 PM. These two boardings decide whether we reach Haldwani at 6:00 PM.
